Got Questions About Solar?

Everything you need to know about tariffs, subsidies, maintenance, and net metering.

Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers receive direct bank transfer (DBT) subsidies: ₹33,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹66,000 for a 2 kW system, and up to ₹78,000 for systems 3 kW and above. SunShell Power is an empanelled vendor and manages your entire portal registration, DISCOM net-meter inspection, and subsidy credit.

SunShell uses advanced Monocrystalline PERC and TOPCon panels that absorb diffused solar radiation. Even during heavy overcast monsoon days, the panels generate 30% to 50% of peak capacity. Furthermore, with an On-Grid Net-Metered system, any shortfall is automatically drawn from the grid with zero power interruption.

SunShell Power provides Tier-1 solar modules with a 25-Year Linear Power Generation Warranty (guaranteeing over 80% output after 25 years) and a 10-Year Comprehensive Inverter Warranty.

A bidirectional net meter records both the electricity you import from the grid and the excess solar electricity your panels export back to the grid during the daytime. At the end of the billing cycle, you only pay for the net units consumed, often reducing your bill to the mandatory minimum fixed charge!

Commercial and Industrial establishments can claim 40% Accelerated Depreciation in the very first year under the Income Tax Act, significantly lowering corporate tax liability. The return on investment (ROI) for industrial solar typically ranges between 2.5 to 3.5 years.
